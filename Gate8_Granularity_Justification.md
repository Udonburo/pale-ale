# Gate8 Granularity Justification

Status: draft skeleton
Role: working justification spec, not final theorem
Date: 2026-03-19

## 0. Purpose

This document exists to define the Gate8 justification burden around mixed granularity.

The immediate question is not:

- how to fuse token and transition scores

The immediate question is:

- why a fixed Gate8 court may legitimately compare token-labeled candidates and a transition-labeled candidate
- under what conditions that comparison remains scientifically honest
- what evidence would be required before any stronger claim is allowed

## 1. Working Thesis

The working thesis for this branch is:

- token-dimensional observation and transition-dimensional observation are not rival claimants to one scalar truth
- they are different observational cuts through the same conflict process
- in conflict-heavy settings, transition-dimensional observation may be closer to the structural object of interest than token-dimensional observation

More sharply:

- token labels ask where local defect is visible at a point
- transition labels ask where failure becomes visible in motion, transport, or step-to-step change

Gate8 should therefore test whether:

- conflict geometry is better exposed by transition observation
- while token observation remains necessary as a guardrail and comparison surface

## 2. Non-Claims

This document does not claim:

- that token and transition labels are interchangeable
- that mixed-granularity comparison is automatically fair
- that `gate7c` has already earned mainline replacement
- that quietness has been won
- that any score fusion or aggregation rescue is licensed
- that field ontology is unlocked

## 3. Why This Question Exists

Up to Gate7c, the project learned:

- dynamic signal is real
- `gate7c` is the strongest dynamic candidate so far
- the win is not clean enough to unlock field language

Gate8 then showed:

- `gate7c` keeps a conflict-side standing gain in a more natural conflict court
- this survives the quietness-pairing repair
- the remaining strongest objection is no longer the quietness bug
- the remaining strongest objection is mixed label granularity

So the next burden is not candidate discovery.

It is doctrinal:

- what exactly is being compared
- why that comparison is allowed
- what would make it illegitimate

## 4. Proposed Distinction

### 4.1 Token Plane

Token-plane labels are appropriate when the observational question is:

- where does the answer surface locally exhibit supported versus defective content
- where is defect readable as a pointwise or near-pointwise mark

Token-plane readouts remain important for:

- support localization
- seam quietness
- guardrail comparison against older score families

### 4.2 Transition Plane

Transition-plane labels are appropriate when the observational question is:

- where does incompatibility become legible in motion rather than position
- where does the trajectory fail to compose cleanly
- where is defect better described as a bad transition than a bad point

Transition-plane readouts are therefore plausible primary observables for:

- distributed incompatibility
- conflict-following wrong continuation
- closure-style failure that emerges across steps

### 4.3 Gate8 Interpretation

The Gate8 working interpretation to test is:

- conflict cells may privilege transition-plane observables
- quietness and local support checks still require token-plane accountability

If true, mixed granularity is not a bug by itself.

It is a statement that:

- different candidates are nearest to different observational slices
- and the court is intentionally testing which slice better tracks conflict geometry

## 5. Gate8 Justification Burden

For this mixed court to remain valid, the following must be made explicit.

### 5.1 Court Metadata

- every candidate must declare `label_key`
- every candidate must declare `label_granularity`
- every execution artifact must declare the court as mixed-granularity

### 5.2 Scope Discipline

- the candidate set remains frozen to `F`, `gate6f`, `gate6h`, `gate7c`
- no new metrics are introduced to rescue the story
- no aggregation is introduced to blur granularity differences

### 5.3 Reading Discipline

Allowed sentence:

- transition observation may be more conflict-relevant on this court

Forbidden sentence:

- transition and token results are now directly commensurate in a fully settled way

## 6. What Must Be Shown Next

The next evidence should target justification, not decoration.

Minimum targets:

1. show that the mixed-granularity caveat is explicit in all tracked artifacts
2. show that the `gate7c` conflict-side gain survives under that explicit court
3. show that quietness remains a separate token-plane guardrail, not something hand-waved away
4. explain why `direct_contradiction` and `distributed_incompatibility` are structurally plausible transition-first cells

### 6.1 Bridge Obligation

The mixed court must not graduate on documentation and persistence alone.

Before this note can move from skeleton to accepted justification, it must lock at least one
falsifiable bridge obligation that could in principle disconfirm the transition-first story.

At minimum, the bridge must be:

- stated before the read is narrated
- tied to named Gate8 cells rather than to a vague global story
- capable of producing a negative result that weakens or kills the doctrine

### 6.2 Allowed First Bridge Families

Two acceptable first bridge families are:

- a per-cell necessity matrix bound to the conflict taxonomy:
  - declare in advance which cells are expected to be transition-first, token-first, or mixed-accountability
  - if the observed standing does not match that matrix, the doctrine weakens
- a same-benchmark dual-label or dual-read check:
  - construct one court where token-plane and transition-plane reads can both be evaluated on the same benchmark support
  - if the transition-first advantage does not survive that bridge, the doctrine weakens

These are examples, not eternal choices.

The real rule is:

- mixed granularity must face at least one bridge that can fail honestly

## 7. Possible Acceptance Rule

This branch could justify the Gate8 mixed court if all of the following hold:

- conflict-side standing for `gate7c` persists under fixed court reruns
- quietness remains tracked independently and is not erased from the decision surface
- the repo never presents token and transition labels as secretly identical
- at least one falsifiable bridge obligation has been locked to the taxonomy or benchmark and remains capable of disproving the transition-first reading
- the justification stays local to Gate8 and does not overclaim ontology

So the court does not close merely because:

- the caveat is explicit
- the rerun is persistent
- quietness is separately tracked

Those are necessary.

They are not sufficient without a real bridge burden.

## 8. Failure Conditions

This justification attempt fails if:

- the argument only works by rhetorical inflation
- mixed granularity is used to excuse contradictory readouts without discipline
- transition advantage disappears when the court is made explicit
- token-plane quietness objections are treated as irrelevant instead of separate
- no falsifiable bridge obligation is ever locked
- the supposed bridge can be rewritten after the fact so that it cannot really fail

## 9. Open Questions

- Is `label_transition` the right transition object, or only the first workable proxy?
- Which conflict cells are genuinely transition-first, and which remain token-first?
- Does a later same-regime bridge become possible without collapsing the distinction?
- Can a future benchmark produce both token and transition labels for the same candidate family without story repair?

## 10. Skeleton Outcome

This document should eventually become one of two things:

- a narrow Gate8 justification note that licenses mixed-granularity reading under explicit caveats
- or a falsified draft that records why the mixed court was not scientifically clean enough

Until then, its only job is to keep the next move honest.
