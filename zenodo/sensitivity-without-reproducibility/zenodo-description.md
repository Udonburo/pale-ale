# Sensitivity Without Reproducibility

**A Measurement Boundary for an Operator-Valued Representation Instrument in Qwen Models**

DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.22180751](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22180751)

Technical report · Not peer reviewed

This study asks when an internal representation observable is reusable across
fresh local presentations rather than merely sensitive to a controlled
perturbation on one substrate. We evaluated a phase-indexed binary register in
a frozen Qwen checkpoint panel, separating explicit transition following,
demonstration-conditioned register behavior, visible-state intervention use,
operator-valued representation measurement, and representation--function
coupling.

Qwen3.5-27B and Qwen3.6-27B passed the behavioral ladder through visible
overwrite propagation; Qwen3-14B and Qwen3.8-27B failed an upstream transition
gate and were not tested downstream. On Qwen3.6-27B, a rank-4 local-frame
paired ridge path-operator packet qualified on one bounded substrate. A
subsequent prospectively frozen 20-block requalification retained
broken-square sensitivity in 59 of 60 layer-blocks, but split-half path-map
spectral re-estimation passed in only 35 of 60 layer-blocks. Only 5 of 20
blocks passed jointly at all three frozen layers, below the required 16 of 20
blocks and four blocks per rollout depth.

Stage E therefore remained unopened. No functional outcome, association,
coefficient, predictive increment, or permutation-test result was collected.
The result is a measurement boundary for this particular rank-4, three-layer,
paired-map route. It does not establish the absence of hidden state, internal
naturality, useful representation geometry, or a neural causal-transition
operator.

The record contains the 13-page DOI-bearing technical report, buildable LaTeX
source, and one model-forward-free reproducibility capsule with all 60
continuous Stage M layer-block records, deterministic figure/table inputs,
model/runtime bindings, provenance closure, licenses, and checksums.

## Scope boundary

The report is limited to the frozen Qwen checkpoints, visible-state
intervention family, rank-4 paired ridge coordinate maps, layers 21/43/62, and
fresh 20-block requalification described in the manuscript. Stage E, hidden
intervention, internal exact naturality, and representation--function coupling
remain untested.
