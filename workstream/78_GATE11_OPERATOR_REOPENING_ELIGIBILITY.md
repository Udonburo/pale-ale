## 0. Scope

Gate11 is not an operator chapter.

Gate11 is:

- a court for whether a real named operator-pressure case exists at all
- a court for whether operator-adjacent reopening may even become eligible later

It does not:

- reopen operator admission
- declare graph-wide operator behavior earned
- settle broader trusted-tree settlement
- retroactively reinterpret Gate9 or Gate10 memory
- promote KAGAMI or higher-obstruction language into the public verdict

It does:

- ask what counts as a real operator-pressure case under the frozen post-Gate10 line
- ask what would have to become true before operator-adjacent reopening is even eligible to be asked again
- preserve the Gate10 bounded closeout sentence
- keep the operator line in eligibility language rather than promotion language

## 1. Public Question

The Gate11 question is:

- `does a real named operator-pressure case exist at all under the frozen post-Gate10 line, and if so what would have to become true before operator-adjacent reopening is even eligible to be asked again?`

This is narrower than:

- operator reopening
- graph-wide operator retry
- field or sheaf promotion

This is also stricter than:

- asking for one more broader trusted-tree settlement slice

## 2. Why Gate11 Exists

Gate10 earned:

- three declared narrow slice-local `settled` results
- bounded broader-pattern support under the preserved Gate10 court
- bounded closeout sentence support

Gate10 did not earn:

- broader trusted-tree settlement as a whole
- operator admission reopening
- retroactive reinterpretation of Gate9 or Gate10 memory

So the next honest chapter is not:

- operator reopening
- a graph-wide operator retry
- theory inflation

It is:

- an eligibility court that fixes what evidence and boundaries would have to exist before operator-adjacent reopening becomes askable again

## 3. Eligibility Target

Gate11 does not ask whether the operator line is right.

It asks whether there is a real named pressure case that the current bounded line cannot honestly host.

Only after that may Gate11 ask whether there is a doctrine-safe path for even reopening the question.

## 4. Frozen Baseline

Any Gate11 implementation must preserve:

- Gate10 closeout as already earned
- broader trusted-tree settlement still unearned
- operator admission still denied
- non-retroactive treatment of Gate9 and Gate10 reads

The frozen baseline sentence is:

- bounded broader-pattern support exists, but broader trusted-tree settlement remains unearned and operator admission remains denied

## 5. Admissible Operator-Pressure Classes

Gate11 may consider a pressure case admissible only if it falls into at least one of the following classes.

### 5.1 Tree-Choice Instability

Admissible pressure may exist if:

- trusted-tree / residual-chord partition choice cannot be kept stable within declared topology hygiene
- the instability is named as an object-level or artifact-level pressure rather than a rhetorical discomfort

### 5.2 Current Bounded Line Insufficiency

Admissible pressure may exist if:

- the current bounded Gate10 line cannot host a named pressure without loss of doctrine discipline
- the insufficiency is about expressive or structural limitation, not impatience or appetite for a stronger theory

### 5.3 Nonlocal Reconciliation Pressure

Admissible pressure may exist if:

- narrow slice-local settlement survives, but a named cross-slice reconciliation pressure cannot be honestly written inside the current bounded line
- the pressure is preserved without promoting broader trusted-tree settlement

### 5.4 Narrow Reopening Pressure Without Graph-Wide Leap

Admissible pressure may exist if:

- a genuinely narrow operator-adjacent reopening shape can be described
- but the current bounded line cannot host that shape without distortion
- the proposed pressure does not require a graph-wide leap

## 6. Inadmissible Pressure Forms

The following do not count as admissible operator-pressure.

### 6.1 Rhetorical Operator Desire

This does not count:

- wanting a stronger language
- wanting a cleaner theory
- wanting to revisit operator ideas without a named pressure object

### 6.2 Settlement Inflation

This does not count:

- promoting bounded broader support into broader trusted-tree settlement
- using settlement inflation as a substitute for a named pressure case

### 6.3 Retroactive Rewrite Pressure

This does not count:

- claiming a pressure case exists only by rewriting Gate9 or Gate10 memory
- re-describing prior reads until a gap appears

### 6.4 Graph-Wide Leap Pressure

This does not count:

- any proposed pressure that only makes sense by sneaking graph-wide operator behavior back in
- field-style rescue dressed up as narrow reopening

## 7. Eligibility Conditions

Gate11 may treat reopening as eligible only if all of the following become true under the frozen court.

### 7.1 A Real Named Pressure Case Exists

Eligibility requires:

- at least one admissible operator-pressure class is instantiated
- the pressure is named as an object-level or artifact-level case
- the pressure is not merely rhetorical

### 7.2 The Current Bounded Line Is Shown Insufficient

Eligibility requires:

- the current bounded line cannot honestly host the named pressure
- this insufficiency is named directly rather than inferred from the number of failed remedies

### 7.3 Settlement Ceiling Remains Explicit

Eligibility requires:

- broader trusted-tree settlement remains explicitly unearned unless separately earned
- no silent conversion of bounded support into broader settlement

### 7.4 Reopening Would Remain Narrow

Eligibility requires:

- any hypothetical reopening remains narrow, falsifier-first, and non-promotional
- no graph-wide operator leap

## 8. Falsifiers

Gate11 denies eligibility if any of the following happens.

### 8.1 No Admissible Named Pressure Exists

Eligibility is denied if:

- no admissible operator-pressure class is instantiated as a named case

### 8.2 Reopening Depends On Settlement Inflation

Eligibility is denied if:

- the reopening case works only by promoting bounded broader support into broader trusted-tree settlement

### 8.3 Reopening Depends On Retroactive Rewrite

Eligibility is denied if:

- the reopening case requires rewriting Gate9 or Gate10 memory rather than proceeding from their frozen boundaries

### 8.4 Reopening Is Graph-Wide By Stealth

Eligibility is denied if:

- the proposed reopening implicitly reintroduces graph-wide operator behavior, smoothing, or field-style rescue

## 9. Forbidden

The following remain forbidden in Gate11:
