## 0. Scope

Gate10 is not an operator chapter.

Gate10 is:

- a settlement court for the broader trusted-tree / residual-chord line beyond the adopted forward-basis split

It does not:

- reopen operator admission
- open a graph-wide operator
- retroactively reinterpret prior Gate9 reads
- settle the entire broader trusted-tree line by declaration
- reopen scalar rescue
- expand the benchmark matrix

It does:

- ask whether trusted-tree / residual-chord decomposition can earn doctrine-safe settlement beyond the adopted forward-basis split
- fix falsifiers before implementation
- preserve the Gate9 discipline while testing broader tree-line generalization

## 1. Public Question

The Gate10 question is:

- `can the trusted-tree / residual-chord line earn doctrine-safe settlement beyond the adopted forward-basis split, without reopening operator admission or rewriting Gate9 memory?`

This is narrower than:

- graph-wide operator reopening
- general tree-line victory
- higher-obstruction or sheaf promotion

It is wider than:

- the already adopted Gate9N forward-basis split

## 2. Why Gate10 Exists

Gate9 earned:

- graph-gauge constitution
- blocker naming at object and artifact level
- one decisive blocker line carried through separability, adoption-worthiness, adopt judgment, and forward-basis integration

Gate9 did not earn:

- broader trusted-tree settlement
- operator admission
- retroactive reinterpretation of earlier reads

So the next honest chapter is not:

- one more audit-lane integration
- one more post-hoc trusted-tree slogan

It is:

- a court that decides whether the broader trusted-tree / residual-chord line can survive as doctrine-safe mainline structure

## 3. Settlement Target

Gate10 does not ask whether the adopted split works.

That was already decided on a forward basis in Gate9P/Q.

Gate10 asks whether the trusted-tree / residual-chord line can generalize beyond that adopted split while preserving:

- doctrine discipline
- cleaner/conflict interpretability
- audit-lane boundary discipline

The settlement target is therefore:

- broader trusted-tree / residual-chord settlement beyond the adopted forward-basis split

## 4. Topology Hygiene Note

Gate10 may use tree-decomposition-like topology hygiene to define trusted-tree / residual-chord partitions deterministically.

This is not a downgrade of geometric resolution.

It is:

- a discipline for deciding where transport defect is measured without ad hoc fill-in

Graph topology organizes the base space.

Transport observables remain:

- gauge-side objects

So Gate10 may borrow graph-theoretic vocabulary for partition hygiene, but it does not become:

- a graph-theory chapter

## 5. Graph / Gauge Boundary Note

The Gate10 boundary remains:

- graph structure decides where trusted-tree and residual-chord partitions are declared
- projector / transport observables decide what geometric defect is measured on those declared structures

Gate10 therefore does not allow:

- scalar masking to replace gauge-side observables
- purely topological verdicts without transport-side reads

## 6. Falsifiers

Gate10 denies settlement if any of the following happens.

### 6.1 No Generalization Beyond The Adopted Split

Settlement is denied if:

- the broader trusted-tree line cannot generalize beyond the adopted forward-basis split

In that case, the Gate9N split remains useful, but broader settlement is not earned.

### 6.2 Side Collapse Under Generalization

Settlement is denied if:

- cleaner-side gain survives only by collapsing conflict-side or quietness-side discipline

Gate10 is not allowed to buy broader tree-line settlement by silently damaging one court to save another.

### 6.3 Ad Hoc Carve-Out Required

Settlement is denied if:

- broader tree-line survival requires ad hoc carve-out, undeclared fill-in, bundle-specific exception logic, or silent broadening of tree semantics

This includes:

- non-declared partition surgery
- special-case residual handling that exists only to preserve the desired verdict

### 6.4 Operator-Adjacent Rescue Required

Settlement is denied if:

- broader standing can be maintained only by reopening operator-adjacent rescue

Gate10 is not allowed to earn settlement by smuggling in:

- graph-wide operator behavior
- operator-like smoothing
- hidden field-style rescue

- no operator admission

- no retroactive rewrite of Gate9

- no silent broadening of trusted-tree semantics

## 8. Non-Promotion Clause

Even if Gate10 produces a strong read, it does not immediately earn:

- mainline replacement
- theory inflation
- operator reopening
- broad theoretical promotion beyond the earned sentence

Gate10 may earn only:

- a settlement sentence
- a bounded-keep sentence
- a denial sentence
- or a defer sentence

## 9. Outcome Ladder

Gate10 outcomes are limited to these four.

### 9.1 Settled

Use `settled` only if:

- the broader trusted-tree / residual-chord line survives doctrine-safe expansion beyond the adopted split
- no falsifier fires

### 9.2 Bounded Keep

Use `bounded keep` if:

- the broader line remains informative or practically useful
- but does not earn doctrine-safe settlement
- while the already integrated Gate9N/P/Q forward-basis split remains preserved rather than relitigated

### 9.3 Denied

Use `denied` if:

- broader settlement fails under the declared court
- and the line should not be casually reopened without a new named blocker or a new constitutional reason

### 9.4 Deferred

Use `deferred` if:

- the evidence remains too incomplete for settlement
- but denial is not yet justified
