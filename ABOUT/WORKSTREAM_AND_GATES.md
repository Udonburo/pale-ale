# Workstream and Gates

A **Gate** is the project's historical name for a research checkpoint: the
question studied, the evidence obtained, and the limits of the conclusion.
The numbered **Workstream** files explain how those checkpoints were reached.

These documents are research history. Names such as constitution, audit,
freeze, authorization, and closeout do not prescribe a development workflow.
A new experiment needs a clear scientific question and method; it does not need
another sequence of permission documents.

Start with the [publication catalog](../publications/README.md) for published
findings and the [workstream index](../workstream/README.md) for historical
context. Read individual notes when they answer a specific question. There is
no need to traverse the numbered history before working on current code.

Historical statements about untested claims remain useful context, but they
are not a queue of unresolved operational obligations.
