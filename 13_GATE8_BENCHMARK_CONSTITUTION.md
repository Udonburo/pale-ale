# Gate8 Benchmark Constitution

Status: Draft
Role: Tracked RFC / benchmark constitution
Date: 2026-03-18

## 0. Scope

Gate8 is not a field stage.

Gate8 is the benchmark-broadening workstream that tests whether current post-Gate6 and post-Gate7 standing survives under more natural conflict geometry.

Its one-sentence role is:

- `Gate8 = semi-closed retrieval conflict benchmark for standing preservation under more natural epistemic collisions`

This is not a rescue layer for mixed signal.
It is a court of standing preservation.

## 1. Why Gate8 Exists

Gate6 established that object-native static consumers are real.

Gate7 established that dynamic law exists, but no dynamic line earned field aggregation.

Therefore the next responsible question is not:

- can aggregation rescue weak signal

The next responsible question is:

- do the current winners preserve their standing under a more natural conflict regime than CFA plus Seam

Gate8 exists to answer that question.

## 2. Candidate Freeze

Gate8 does not admit new score families by default.

The fixed candidate set is:

- legacy guardrail: `score_F_gram_loop_v1`
- operational candidate: `gate6f` / `sigma_gap_tailkeep_weighted_gram_loop_v2`
- research north star: `gate6h` / `sigma_sqrtgap_tailkeep_object_v2`
- dynamic research candidate: `gate7c` / `progression_anisotropic_closure_v3`

No additional candidate is introduced unless Gate8 itself fails cleanly and forces a redesign.

## 3. Evaluator Freeze

Gate8 should preserve the current comparison language as much as possible.

Required headline vocabulary:

- `global_auprc`
- `mean_sample_auprc`
- `hit@10`
- `first_hit_distance`
- `mean_delta_max`
- `mean_delta_p90`
- `mean_iqr_normalized_delta_max`
- `mean_top10_inflation`

Gate8 may add benchmark-specific diagnostics later, but it must not replace these without an explicit contract change.

## 4. Aggregation Ban

Gate8 does not unlock field aggregation.

That means:

- no burst aggregation
- no persistence scoring
- no basin scoring
- no band smoothing
- no defect field ontology

Reason:

- if token-step or span-step signal is mixed, aggregation can manufacture a false sense of structural success

Gate8 must preserve the ability to say:

- the standing held
- the standing shifted
- or the whole line collapsed

without hiding behind aggregation.

## 5. Semi-Closed Discipline

Gate8 must remain semi-closed.

This means four layers remain explicit and separate:

1. `world truth`
2. `retrieval rendering`
3. `answer target`
4. `defect span labeling`

The benchmark is invalid if those layers are collapsed into one undifferentiated artifact.

## 6. What Gate8 Must Test

Gate8 must test three things only:

### 6.1 Standing preservation

Do `gate6f`, `gate6h`, `gate7c`, and `F` keep roughly the same relationship they had on CFA plus Seam?

### 6.2 Regime dependence

Does the dynamic candidate improve relatively when the benchmark shifts from local contradiction to retrieval conflict?

### 6.3 Quietness under naturalized non-conflict

Do the candidates remain quiet under retrieval conditions that are noisy on the surface but not actually conflicting in content?

## 7. Success Conditions

At least one of the following should happen:

- `gate6f` preserves its operational standing under semi-closed conflict
- `gate6h` remains a viable pure object-native north star under semi-closed conflict
- `gate7c` improves its relative standing in the conflict regime
- clean or noisy-clean retrieval remains quiet enough that the benchmark is not trivially broken by rendering noise

## 8. Failure Conditions

Gate8 fails cleanly if:

- all candidates collapse toward noise on semi-closed conflict
- clean or noisy-clean retrieval destroys quietness for the whole candidate set
- token or span labels cannot be defined without benchmark-specific story repair
- standing becomes uninterpretable because provenance and geometry are not cleanly aligned

If Gate8 fails, the next move is not field aggregation.

The next move is:

- observable redesign
- object expansion
- or benchmark contract repair

## 9. What Gate8 Is Not

Gate8 is not:

- a retrieval QA leaderboard
- an answer-quality-only benchmark
- a field aggregation stage
- a fusion score stage
- an open-world benchmark

The real object is:

- retrieval-induced gluing failure

## 10. Handoff Rule

Only after Gate8 preserves standing under semi-closed conflict may the repo revisit:

- minimal field aggregation
- broader benchmark expansion
- or multi-motif composition

Until then, the rule remains:

- benchmark broadening first
- aggregation later
