# Local Mapping Without Iterative Closure

**Zero-Shot and Input-Output-Only In-Context Graph-XOR Capability Boundaries in Qwen3**

DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21992852

Frozen-protocol pilot study · Not peer reviewed

We evaluated zero-shot and input-output-only in-context graph-XOR capabilities
in Qwen3 models from 0.6B to 8B under prospectively frozen, bounded protocols. In zero-shot
official non-thinking mode, reliable behavior reached a one-edge relation at
8B but not a two-input XOR behavioral pass. Under the joint
correct-versus-label-shuffled formation criterion, the two-input mapping
surface first qualified at 16 demonstrations in Qwen3-4B and at 4
demonstrations in Qwen3-8B. Correct-demonstration cells passed behaviorally at
16 and 64 shots in 4B and at 4, 16, and 64 shots in 8B; only 16 and 64 in 4B
and 4 in 8B satisfied the joint formation contrast. No correct-demonstration
length-8 parity cell met the predeclared score-signal criterion on the frozen
ledgers.

The observed boundary therefore lies between context-conditioned local mapping
acquisition and iterative composition. In the ICL branches, shuffled
relational-cycle evaluation remained unopened after parity failed to qualify;
the zero-shot scout separately measured ordered and shuffled relational-cycle
surfaces and found no detected signal. Multi-cycle global obstruction remained
unevaluated. The result does not establish a
reusable XOR algorithm, a specific internal state-tracking mechanism, or
absence of hidden information. A2-M, decorated-theta B, activation extraction,
naturality, and causal intervention remained unopened by conditional stopping.
Each ICL branch used one prospectively fixed demonstration bank and order;
bank-to-bank and order-to-order variance were not assessed.

The record contains one integrated 7-page technical report, a publication-ready
capability matrix, and one reproducibility capsule with public-safe plans,
runners, exact result summaries, cell-level metrics, model/runtime bindings,
and checksums. It is a standalone graph-XOR capability-boundary report, not a
new version of the author's replay/artifact-structure reports.

## Suggested keywords

graph-XOR; parity; in-context learning; Qwen3; capability boundary; frozen

## Scope boundary

The report closes only the tested Qwen3 0.6B–8B zero-shot official
non-thinking and bounded input-output-only ICL graph-XOR route. It does not
test thinking mode, generated scratchpads, fine-tuning, models above 8B, other
families, hidden-state information, naturality, causal use, or broader
hypotheses about latent global-obstruction representations.
