Primary result
--------------

The study separated visible-state use, bounded operator-instrument
qualification, fresh cross-presentation re-estimation, and a conditional
representation--function analysis. Qwen3.5-27B and Qwen3.6-27B qualified for
visible overwrite propagation on one frozen register protocol. A rank-4,
three-layer paired-map operator instrument then qualified on one bounded
Qwen3.6-27B substrate.

In a prospectively frozen 20-block requalification, the broken-square control
remained detectable in 59 of 60 layer-blocks, while split-half path-map
spectral re-estimation passed in 35 of 60 layer-blocks. Only 5 of 20 blocks
passed jointly at all three frozen layers, below the required 16 of 20 blocks
and four blocks at each rollout depth. Stage E therefore remained unopened;
no representation--function association was estimated.

The result closes this particular rank-4, three-layer, paired-map measurement
route under the frozen protocol. It does not establish the absence of hidden
state, internal naturality, useful representation geometry, or a causal
transition operator.
