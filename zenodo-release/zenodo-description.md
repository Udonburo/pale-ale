Structural Replay in Dense Transformers Under a Frozen FP32 Regime: Evidence
from Closed 3B/4B Models and Boundary Results

Under a frozen FP32 dense-transformer regime, this report shows that a Gate12A
structural replay signature persists across the closed 3B/4B full-family-set
lines. Within the present first-pass taxonomy, phenotype remains
family-conditioned and model-specific, with the clearest limited convergence
appearing at archive high tension inside the current closed 3B/4B evidence
set.

Separate transcript-only observations, including an imported Mistral-7B line,
broaden the structural range without being treated as mainline phenotype
evidence. Boundary checks further show that some candidates fail
frozen-protocol admission, while a non-transformer sidecar (Mamba 2.8B under
an altered observation surface) does not preserve the current
dense-transformer transcript path signature.
